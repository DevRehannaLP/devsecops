# Cover Letter Run Down

- 🧠 Why This Cover Letter Works (The Strategy)
  - The Hook (Paragraph 1): It immediately addresses the elephant in the room
    (career transition) and frames it as a massive advantage. "Military-grade
    discipline" and "security-first mindset" are music to a hiring manager's ears.
    - Is a massive green flag. It proves you aren't just reading books; you are
      actively building and managing code like a professional.
  - The Bridge (Paragraph 3): This connects your past to your future. It
    explains why a former helicopter mechanic and patient triage specialist will
    be an amazing SysAdmin: you know how to handle pressure, follow strict
    compliance (HIPAA translates well to security policies), and troubleshoot systematically.
  - The Close (Paragraph 4): It states your long-term goal (DevSecOps) without
    making it sound like you'll leave the SysAdmin role in 3 months. It frames it
    as "I want to grow with you."
